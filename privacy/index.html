Underlye

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Underlye has no user accounts and does not keep your data on its own servers. Your library, quotes, notes and sessions live on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your iCloud account. Only a few specific pieces of data leave your phone, to search for books, recommend reads and manage your subscription, and they do not identify you.

Data controller

Mateo Iglesias Pedroviejo, Spain. Contact: underlyeapp@gmail.com

We never send your name, quotes, notes or reflections to any server. The camera is used to scan barcodes and read text from a page; this is processed on your iPhone and no photos are uploaded. Reading reminders are local notifications on your phone.

We use no advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share data with third parties.

Legal basis

We process this data because it is necessary to provide the service you subscribe to (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). The anonymous install ID is also used to prevent abuse of recommendations, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the service running (Article 6(1)(f)).

Service providers

  • Apple: iCloud (sync) and the App Store (payments).
  • Supabase (server in the EU, Ireland): receives the recommendation request and does not store it.
  • Anthropic (United States): generates recommendations with its AI model from your library summary. Under its commercial terms, it does not use this data to train its models.
  • Google Books (Google) and Open Library (Internet Archive): book search and covers.
  • RevenueCat (United States): subscription management.

Where a provider is outside the European Economic Area, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

How long data is kept

Data on your iPhone and in iCloud is kept until you delete it. Our server does not store recommendation requests; it only counts how many each install made that day and clears the count the next day. RevenueCat keeps purchase history as long as needed to manage the subscription and meet legal obligations.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all my data removes everything the app has on your iPhone and in your iCloud. You can also delete it from your iPhone's iCloud settings. Your subscription is cancelled separately, in your Apple account settings.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction and portability by writing to underlyeapp@gmail.com. Since almost everything stays on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. If you believe we are not handling your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

Children

Underlye is not directed at children under 14.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will let you know in the app and update the date above.
